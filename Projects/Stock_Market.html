01
THE QUESTION

How do you turn a stream of headlines into market context?

Financial information moves quickly, but finding the signal inside hundreds of headlines can be surprisingly difficult.

Financial news is distributed across multiple publishers, feeds and categories. Reading every article individually makes it difficult to understand the broader tone of the market in real time.

Traditional financial dashboards can also make current news feel disconnected from the sentiment surrounding it. A headline may be positive, negative or neutral long before its broader market implications become obvious.

News Pulse was built around a simple principle: collect the information, analyze its tone and make the overall signal visible.

THESIS

The goal is not to replace financial research. It is to create a faster way to scan financial news, identify sentiment and understand what themes are dominating the current information flow.

ERROR ANALYSIS

Where automated news reading gets difficult.

Financial language is contextual, and a sentiment model can miss meaning that a human reader would immediately recognize.
01

Financial Language

Specialized financial terminology can carry meanings that are not always captured correctly by general-purpose sentiment scoring.

02

Context Dependence

A headline may contain both positive and negative information, making a single sentiment label an incomplete representation.

03

Feed Reliability

RSS structures and publisher availability can change, creating dependencies outside the sentiment model itself.

12
LIMITATIONS

Knowing what sentiment analysis cannot explain.

01

VADER Context

VADER is a general-purpose sentiment tool and may not fully understand specialized financial terminology or market context.

02

RSS Dependency

The application depends on external RSS feeds whose availability, structure and content can change over time.

03

Headline Only

Sentiment is primarily derived from headline text, so important context contained inside the full article may not be represented.

04

Interpretation

A positive or negative headline does not automatically imply a corresponding movement in an asset or financial market.

IMPORTANT

News sentiment is an analytical signal, not a trading recommendation. The dashboard is designed for research, exploration and information discovery.

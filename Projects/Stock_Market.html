THE QUESTION

What happens when the notebook ends?

Most forecasting projects can produce impressive historical metrics. The harder question is whether those models remain useful when the data keeps moving.

Zephyr was built around one central question:

Which models still hold up when they leave the notebook and face live market data?

Instead of treating a strong historical score as the finish line, the system compares models under the same evaluation conditions and exposes their strengths and failures.

The project therefore connects two worlds: detailed offline experimentation and a live forecasting environment that can retrain models, compare results and incorporate current market context.

01

Historical optimism

Models can look strong when evaluated only against historical observations.

02

Changing conditions

Market behaviour changes, making consistent live evaluation essential.

03

Transparent failure

Underperformance is retained as evidence rather than hidden behind a single score.

FEATURE ENGINEERING

Teaching the models what to notice.

Price alone is only one signal. Zephyr constructs a broader representation of market behaviour from returns, trend, volatility, momentum and volume.

The research notebook generates a structured feature space designed for cross-model comparison.

Features include:

Daily Return · MA7 · MA30 · MA90 · 30-day Volatility · Lag Close · Momentum · Price Position · Volume Ratio

Additional market-structure features include high-low range, typical price, overnight gap and target direction.

01

Trend

MA7, MA30 and MA90 capture short-, medium- and longer-term price movement.

02

Momentum

Five-day and ten-day momentum expose recent directional behaviour.

03

Volatility

Rolling volatility and high-low range provide context around market instability.

04

Volume

Volume moving averages and ratios add activity-based information to the feature space.

EVALUATION CONTRACT

Five metrics. One backtest window.

Every live model is evaluated using the same metrics so that comparisons remain consistent.

MAE Mean absolute forecasting error
RMSE Error with greater penalty for larger deviations
MAPE Percentage-based forecasting error
R² Explained variance perspective
DIRECTION Direction Accuracy of predicted movement

The purpose is not to manufacture one winning model. It is to understand how different approaches behave under the same conditions.

This makes model failure informative: a model that performs poorly is still useful if its behaviour reveals where an approach breaks down.

MARKET CONTEXT

Numbers tell one story. Headlines add context.

Price forecasts do not exist in isolation. Zephyr adds a lightweight news and sentiment layer to expose the information surrounding the selected stock.

Live news is collected from Investing.com and processed with VADER sentiment analysis.

LIMITATIONS

What the system still cannot see.

Financial markets are noisy, adaptive and affected by information that a historical feature table cannot completely represent.

Zephyr intentionally does not claim perfect prediction. Its purpose is to make model behaviour measurable and transparent.

The README identifies several areas where the system can be extended:

01

Market Regimes

Model selection could become aware of changing market regimes.

02

Uncertainty

Probabilistic forecasts and confidence intervals remain future work.

03

Explainability

Feature-importance and SHAP views could make model decisions easier to inspect.

04

External Events

Earnings, macro releases and other events could be incorporated directly into forecasting.

05

Longer Horizons

Sustained-volatility evaluation over longer forecasting horizons remains an extension.

06

News Resilience

More robust multi-source news fallbacks could strengthen the context layer.

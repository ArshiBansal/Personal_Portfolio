THE QUESTION

Can multiple complementary models produce more stable equity forecasts than any single algorithm?

No single forecasting method dominates across all market regimes.

Classical models capture different patterns from deep learning sequences. Tree-based methods handle non-linear relationships well, while Prophet and recurrent networks specialise in temporal structure. Relying on only one of them leaves the system exposed to the weaknesses of that particular approach.

This platform was designed to run eight complementary models in parallel, combine their outputs through a performance-weighted ensemble, and contextualise the quantitative forecasts with live news sentiment.

THESIS

A carefully weighted ensemble of diverse models can deliver more robust multi-horizon price predictions than any individual algorithm alone.

KEY INSIGHTS

What the ensemble approach delivers.

RESULTS

Stability through diversity.

Combining models with different inductive biases reduces reliance on any single forecasting style and produces more consistent multi-horizon predictions.

01 EIGHT-MODEL COVERAGE

Classical, gradient-boosting, time-series and recurrent models run side-by-side on the same data.

02 WEIGHTED ENSEMBLE

Model contributions are adjusted according to back-tested accuracy rather than equal weighting.

03 BUY–SELL SIGNALS

Ensemble moving-average crossovers generate actionable signals on top of the price forecasts.

04 LIVE SENTIMENT

Real-time news aggregation and VADER scoring place the numerical forecasts in current market context.

05 INTERACTIVE VIEW

Plotly-powered charts and Streamlit controls let users inspect individual model behaviour and ensemble output.

05
LIMITATIONS

Where forecasting systems remain constrained.

01

Market Regime Shifts

Models trained on past regimes can degrade when volatility, correlation structure or liquidity conditions change abruptly.

02

Sentiment Noise

VADER is a general lexicon model and can misread financial jargon, irony or headlines that require deeper context.

03

Look-ahead Risk

Feature engineering and ensemble weighting must be carefully isolated from future information to avoid leakage.

04

No Guarantee of Alpha

Improved forecast stability does not automatically translate into profitable trading after costs and slippage.

IMPORTANT

This platform is a research and decision-support system. It is not a trading bot and does not constitute financial advice.

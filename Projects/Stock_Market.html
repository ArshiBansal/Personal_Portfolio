01
THE QUESTION

Do machine learning and deep learning models improve short-horizon stock forecasts over simple baselines?

Most forecasting projects stop at a single model. Real insight comes from disciplined comparison.

Classical tree-based models, gradient boosting methods and recurrent networks each capture different structures in price data. Without a consistent evaluation framework it is impossible to know whether added complexity actually improves forecasts.

Zephyr was built to run multiple complementary models under the same chronological conditions, measure them against strong naïve baselines, and surface the results through an interactive dashboard.

THESIS

A transparent, leakage-free multi-model pipeline reveals whether model complexity delivers real gains over simple baselines on short-horizon equity returns.

KEY INSIGHTS

What disciplined model comparison reveals.

RESULTS

Complexity does not automatically win.

When models are evaluated fairly against strong baselines using chronological splits, the advantage of more sophisticated algorithms is often smaller than expected.

01 MULTI-MODEL COVERAGE

Classical, gradient-boosting and recurrent models run on the same feature set and time splits.

02 STRONG BASELINES

Naïve last-close and last-return baselines provide a clear reference point for measuring real improvement.

03 CHRONOLOGICAL VALIDATION

All evaluation respects time order. Walk-forward testing is supported for more robust assessment.

04 CLEAR METRICS

MAE, RMSE and Directional Accuracy are reported consistently across every model.

05 INTERACTIVE EXPLORATION

The Streamlit dashboard makes it easy to inspect individual tickers, features, training behaviour and forecast quality.

05
LIMITATIONS

Where forecasting systems remain constrained.

01

Market Regime Shifts

Models trained on past regimes can degrade when volatility, correlation structure or liquidity conditions change abruptly.

02

Low Signal-to-Noise

Short-horizon equity returns are inherently noisy. Even well-built models often achieve only modest directional accuracy.

03

Data Constraints

Yahoo Finance data contains delays, gaps and occasional corporate-action artefacts, especially for non-US listings.

04

No Guarantee of Alpha

Better forecast metrics do not automatically translate into profitable trading after costs, slippage and risk management.

IMPORTANT

This platform is an educational and research system. It is not a trading bot and does not constitute financial advice.
